## Focus Areas for You

Given your Microsoft background, focus on these AWS-specific topics:

- **AWS pricing models** (On-Demand, Reserved, Spot, Savings Plans)
- **Shared Responsibility Model** (you know Microsoft's — compare them)
- **AWS Support Plans** (Basic, Developer, Business, Enterprise)
- **AWS service names** — you know the concepts, just learn the AWS names
- **AWS global infrastructure** (Regions, AZs, Edge Locations)

!!! tip "You have an advantage"
    Your SC-100 means you already understand cloud architecture, security, and compliance. The CCP is mostly about AWS vocabulary and pricing — concepts you can learn in 2–3 weeks.
